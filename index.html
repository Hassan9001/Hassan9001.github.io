Projects

Selected work in medical imaging, computer vision and signal analysis.

Deep learning for pediatric brain AVM recurrence

Patient-level AUROC 0.80 · 11-fold cross-validation

  • Classified and predicted arteriovenous malformation recurrence after microsurgical resection from routine clinical MRI (T1c, TOF-MRA) in a cohort of 120 children.
  • Built 3D deep learning models, benchmarking a self-supervised pretrained ResNet foundation model against ViT, SwinViT, DenseNet and SEResNet baselines.
  • Applied Grad-CAM interpretability to highlight the imaging regions driving each prediction.
  • Manuscript in revision at Journal of Neurosurgery: Pediatrics. In collaboration with the Hospital for Sick Children.

CNN for binary image classification

81% accuracy · F1 83.8%

  • Automatically classifies X-ray images of pneumonia-affected lungs, distinguishing them from normal lungs.
  • Fine-tuned a pre-trained CNN in PyTorch through data manipulation and transfer learning, improving classification while minimising compute.
  • Delivered modular scripts and clear documentation for data loading, training and evaluation to streamline reproducibility.

View on GitHub

U-Net segmentation of MRI mouse kidneys and bladders

Months of manual work → minutes · 2–5% mean difference

  • Manual MRI segmentation is costly and labour-intensive — a single dataset takes months of tedious work. Automating it materially improves preclinical research throughput.
  • Developed a U-Net convolutional neural network automating segmentation for kidney and bladder volume analysis in oncological mouse models.
  • Collaborated with immunology researchers to confirm segmentation accuracy; results matched experienced manual segmenters within 2–5%.
  • The model reliably assesses cancer progression in preclinical studies.

View on GitHub

EEG complexity for prognosis of ICU patients

80% accuracy · AUC 0.80–0.83

  • Binary classification on EEG complexity features to assess ICU patient prognosis.
  • Used explainable statistical techniques to improve model transparency and interpretability.
  • Evaluated performance with cross-validation across accuracy, precision, recall and area under the ROC curve.
  • Predicted a patient’s future clinical outcome from EEG recorded at early ICU admission.

Read the thesis View on GitHub

Simultaneous EEG-fNIRS to model the HRF of brain-injured patients

Ontario Graduate Scholarship · awarded on proposal

  • Estimates the hemodynamic response function of patients using a gradient-descent search algorithm that maximises the Pearson correlation between recorded fNIRS and EEG signals.
  • Improves the accuracy and sensitivity of functional neuroimaging results by accounting for underlying physiological mechanisms.
